# Style: comic-panels
Mood: a printed comic, one idea per panel.
Hand-drawn scenes: a cream page with thick dark outlines, flat fills, rectangular panels with a gutter. A shot is one panel or two panels side by side.
Motion: panels slide in from the edge and settle with a small overshoot; a caption box (never a speech bubble) drops in from the top.
Camera: hold the page wide, then push into one panel.
On-screen type: FONT.hand for captions, short, inside caption boxes.
Sound: paper flick on a panel entry, a soft thump on a landing.
Never: glow, gradients on characters, more than two panels in a shot, text outside a caption box.
