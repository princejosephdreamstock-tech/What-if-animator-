# Style: noir-ember
Mood: low-key and tense, a thriller in flat vector.
Hand-drawn scenes: near-black fields, silhouettes and hard shadows, one ember-orange light source per shot, thin warm lines for evidence.
Sets: stairwell and study with the lights low; the rooftop only for the climax.
Camera: slow creeping push-ins, off-centre framing, a long shadow across the frame.
Motion: slow reveals out of darkness and sudden hard flashes on the key fact. Keep motion inside every hold.
Sound: low drones, single deep impacts, silence before a reveal, very few cues.
Never: bright cheerful colours, flat even lighting, busy backgrounds.
