# Style: blueprint
Mood: an engineer's drawing: calm, exact, a little playful.
Hand-drawn scenes: a deep blue grid field, thin white and cyan line art, labelled nodes joined by lines that draw on, dimension marks and callouts. The one coral accent marks the part that matters.
Sets: schematic first. Characters appear small beside the diagram or in a study shot. At most two character close-ups.
Camera: slow zoom on one node at a time, then pull back to show the whole system.
Motion: lines draw on, boxes snap into the grid, numbers count up.
On-screen type: FONT.ui only, capitals for labels.
Sound: precise ticks and soft whooshes, a low tone when the diagram completes.
Never: warm interior colours, rounded blobby shapes, more than one accent colour.
