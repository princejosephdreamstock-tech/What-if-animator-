# Style: chalk-classroom
Mood: a patient teacher at a chalkboard.
Sets: classroom as home base, with a few study and kitchen shots for the examples.
Hand-drawn scenes: a dark green board, off-white chalk lines with a slightly wobbly edge, steps revealed one at a time, circled answers, tick marks and simple icons.
Camera: stay fairly wide on the board and push in only on the step being explained.
Motion: chalk lines draw on at hand speed; a step is dimmed before the next one starts.
On-screen type: FONT.ui only, short.
Sound: soft chalk taps and a gentle chime when an answer lands. No risers.
Never: neon glow, dark moody lighting, more than one new idea per shot.
