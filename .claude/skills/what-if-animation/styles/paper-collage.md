# Style: paper-collage
Mood: a documentary explainer made of cut paper on a desk.
Paper world: draw every hand-drawn scene on a warm cream panel (about #F1E6D2, never pure white or pure black). The palette background shows only at the frame edges.
Layers: subjects are flat shapes with a soft offset shadow and a thin lighter edge, as if placed on the paper.
Choreography: layers enter one after another (slide, pop with overshoot, rise), then never sit still: a slow drift of a few pixels.
Camera: one oversized board; the camera pushes and pans between anchor points with the engine camera helper, so the scene travels.
Annotation: slightly rotated label chips, marker sweeps behind key words, arrows that draw on. Over any busy layer put a cream strip behind the text.
Sets: kitchen, classroom and rooftop for the character moments; everything else is paper boards.
Sound: paper slides, soft thuds when a cutout lands, a sparse riser before a reveal.
Never: glow, neon, dark panels, text directly over a busy picture.
