# Style: night-desk
Mood: a quiet room at night lit by screens. Cool, focused, a little lonely.
Sets: lead with the study set (night on) and the schematic set; at most one rooftop or stairwell shot as a breather.
Hand-drawn scenes: glowing panels, thin line charts, cursor and notification shapes on a near-black field. One warm accent, used sparingly, for the thing that matters.
Camera: slow push-ins on the panel or the face; pull back to the wide shot for the reveal.
Motion: elements slide in from the screen edge with a short ease, then idle with a faint flicker.
On-screen type: FONT.ui only, short lowercase labels.
Sound: soft clicks and low hums, few cues, one hero hit per reveal.
Never: bright daylight, warm cartoon colours, more than 3 shots in a row on the same set.
