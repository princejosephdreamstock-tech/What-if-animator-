Making an animated video by hand is a pile of small jobs.
Now picture a factory line. One topic line goes in at the left.
First station, the script. Your writing key turns the line into narration.
Second station, the voice. Your voice key records it, and every word gets a time.
Third station, the shot list. Cuts land on the spoken words, and shot sizes keep changing.
Fourth station, the frames. Every picture is drawn in code, then animated.
Fifth station, the sound. Ticks, whooshes and a music bed are built to match the cuts.
Last station, the master. The mix is levelled so the whole video sounds even.
And a finished video rolls off the end of the line, ready to upload.
You get the engine, the cast, the sets, the scripts and the guides.
You bring your own keys for the voice and the writing. No keys come inside.
