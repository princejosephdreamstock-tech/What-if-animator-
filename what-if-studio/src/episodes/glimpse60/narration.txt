What if you could glimpse one minute ahead?
Sounds amazing. Honestly, it does. You win every argument. You dodge every puddle. You ace every test. You're basically a wizard.
But here's the catch. A glimpse isn't free. Your brain is two percent of your body weight, and it burns a fifth of all your energy. Twenty watts, nonstop. About a dim light bulb. Now run a whole minute of life in one second. Every single time you glimpse. Sixty times the power. Twelve hundred watts. A space heater, running inside your skull. That's real.
At first, a headache. A dull, pounding one. Then the world stutters. Like a video that won't load. Then the glimpses won't stop. One after another after another. Your head is hot. Your vision is white. Your ears are ringing. So what happens when the power fails?
